Protect Medford Camera Registry Agreement


By registering your security cameras, you agree to share the location of your cameras with the Medford Police Department for investigative and emergency response purposes. Registration does not provide live access, remote access, or automatic video access to your cameras.


Your registration information is accessible only to authorized Medford Police Department personnel and is used solely for official purposes.


If an incident occurs near your registered location, investigators may contact you directly using the information you provide to request relevant video footage. You choose whether to share footage, and you may update or remove your registration at any time by contacting tiu@cityofmedford.org.
